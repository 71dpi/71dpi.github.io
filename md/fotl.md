# Fruit of the Loom eCommerce UI/UX

**TL;DR**

###### Alex produced wireframes and visual designs for eCommerce websites in the Fruit of the Loom family of brands. Post-launch, the site saw a 142% increase in visitors and 140% increase in revenue in its first month.
