# Snack Pack UI & Content

![Pudding kid from Billy Madison](https://71dpi.github.io/img/projects/snackpack/snackpack01.gif)

Responsive web UX/UI for the brown bag lunch classic, Snack Pack. Key considerations for the site were brand presence, easy access to products and nutrition facts, and content strategy to boost SEO and drive traffic, while making things easy to share on social media.
