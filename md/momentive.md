# Momentive UI

![Person browsing Momentive website on iPad](https://71dpi.github.io/img/projects/momentive/m03.png)

A fully responsive marketing, cataloging and ordering solution for the world's second largest producer of industrial silicones. In a massive effort to streamline business to business connections, Momentive's rebranding was the perfect moment to go from old-school catalogues and a desktop-only website to a feature-rich online experience, along with a revamped visual identity, refined copywriting, and an overhaul to all printed and trade show materials.
