# Good at Business. Family Man. Has Ideas.

## Good Business Boy

I like working on things that help people and make them happy. Sometimes that's a work solution that make's people's jobs easier. Other times it's an attractive logo or t-shirt design. More often it's a Design Thinking exercise, leading teams to solve problems.

You're likely here because you're in the Product, UX, or Design space, or you're looking for help in one of those areas. Check out [my projects](https://71dpi.github.io/md/projects.md), or see my work history using the buttons below, and reach out with any questions.

## Actual Person

Happily married he/him with two lively kids, two even livelier Boston Terriers, and a cat to rule them all. I'm a Christian, but try super hard not to be the judgy kind.

I cook almost every night and love experimenting with flavors from all sorts of cultures. Speaking of cultures, I also brew my own kombucha.

There's usually a podcast or [Spotify playlist](https://open.spotify.com/user/129808580?si=NUYznv5iQPqSngTZGo_QXw) on. I'll listen to just about anything, but typically gravitate toward hyperpop, punk, and rap of the 2010s. If there's a television on, I hope it's showing a cooking show, NFL football, or we're playing Super Smash Bros.
