# Labs 🧪

A space for the things that nobody seems to want to pay me to do.

## Games

Collections of games I've created using Claude as a series of exercises in learning more about prompt engineering / vibe coding. My goals in doing so were to learn and have fun, while crafting a love letter to the [ 90s shovelware CDs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shovelware) of my childhood. Please enjoy.

## Words

A place to express my thoughts and philosophy on design, tech, life and whatever else is on my mind.
