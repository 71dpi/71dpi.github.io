# SunRun app UX/UI

My Sunrun PhoneGap app and site experience for solar energy customers. My Sunrun provides solar customers a way to track their energy production levels, refer others, and share their energy production and consumption statistics via social media. UI elements, iconography and photo selections also made their way back into the company's marketing site and materials.
